A little help with your window.

Raaam translates visible Dutch text into English, with recognition and translation performed on your iPhone.

Quick help

01

Start sharing

Open Raaam, tap the magenta character, then choose Start Broadcast in Apple’s system sheet.

02

Translate

Open content containing Dutch text and move the floating magenta window over the words you want to understand.

03

Tuck Raaam away

Swipe the floating window fully beyond an edge when the screen is moving. Swipe it back when you need it again.

Troubleshooting

Translation is not appearing

Confirm that screen sharing is active and that the floating window is over clear Dutch text. iOS may need to download its Dutch and English language resources before the first translation.

The floating window will not start

Return to Raaam, stop the current broadcast using iOS’s recording indicator, and start again. Restarting the iPhone can also clear a stalled Picture in Picture session.

How do I stop screen sharing?

Tap the recording indicator shown by iOS and choose Stop, or return to Raaam and use the available stop control.

Does Raaam upload my screen?

No. Screen frames, recognised text and translations are processed locally and are not sent to the developer or third parties. Read the privacy policy for details.

Still need help?
