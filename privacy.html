Privacy policy

Your screen stays yours.

Last updated: 23 September 2026

Overview

Raaam is designed to translate visible Dutch text into English without sending screen contents away from the device.

Data collection

Raaam does not collect personal data, create user accounts, include analytics or advertising SDKs, or transmit screen contents, recognised text or translations to the developer or third parties.

Screen processing

Screen sharing begins only after you tap Raaam and confirm Start Broadcast in Apple’s ReplayKit sheet. While sharing, ReplayKit makes the device screen visible to Raaam’s broadcast extension. This may include sensitive content such as notifications, messages, passwords, health information and financial information.

Vision text recognition and Translation processing occur on the device. Apple may download the Dutch and English language resources required by the Translation framework.

Temporary storage

The broadcast extension uses a private App Group container to pass the latest compressed frame to the main app. Only one frame is kept and replaced as the screen changes. Raaam deletes this temporary frame when sharing starts, when sharing ends normally, when the extension exits, and when the app next opens. Recognised text and translations are held in memory for the active app session.

Your control

iOS displays its system recording indicator while screen sharing is active. You can stop sharing through that system indicator.

Contact
